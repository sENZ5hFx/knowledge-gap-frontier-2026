## Primary insight

Bathymetry is the wrong progress bar, and it moved anyway. Seabed 2030 / GEBCO: 27.3% of the ocean floor mapped to modern standards by 21 June 2025 (the figure the October log used); 28.7% (~104 million km²) in the 2026 grid announced 20 April 2026. That is +1.4 percentage points. It does not constrain carbon.

In one well-sampled basin (subarctic Northeast Atlantic, spring 2021, EXPORTS / WHOI Ocean Twilight Zone), mesopelagic fishes contribute somewhere between ~0.52% and ~18% of the biological carbon pump at 200 m. That is about a 35-fold range. Biomass uncertainty and bioenergetic uncertainty contribute about equally. Of the fish-mediated carbon that reaches 200 m, only ~8–30% is sequestered on >100-year scales. Global mesopelagic biomass remains a range (about 2–16 Gt in later syntheses; Irigoien et al. 2014 had already moved the trawl-era ~1 Gt estimate up by at least 10×).

Same shape, other realms, and not previously the lead of this series:

- **Space / ice-ocean.** Enceladus plume H2 (2017) and phosphorus (2023) are chemistry. Europa's "twice Earth's ocean" figure is an interior model. A reanalysis cut confidence in Europa plumes from ~99.9% to under 90%. No life has been shown. WHOI InvOW (selected 3 Dec 2025, starts 2026) exists because organic carbon in plume ice cannot yet be read as biogenic versus abiotic. The sample-interpretation problem arrives before the sample. October's space items were black-hole seeds and the Hubble tension. This is a different gap.
- **Humans.** October's human item was the gut virome (reads that match no genome). This session's human item is bacterial protein function. Even in the best-sampled human microbial habitat, up to ~70% of proteins were uncharacterized. FUGAsseM (Zhang, Bhosle, Bae, Huttenhower et al., Nature Biotechnology, Oct 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41587-025-02813-7) assigned high-confidence *putative* functions to >443,000 families from 1,595 HMP2 gut metagenomes and 800 metatranscriptomes; ~82.3% of those families had been uncharacterized, including >6,000 with no homology. Predictions are not enzymology.
- **Land.** October's fungal item was unnamed ectomycorrhizal species (~83%, secondary). This session's land item is geographic: >70% of terrestrial ecoregions have no arbuscular-mycorrhizal sequencing data (Stewart et al., FEMS Microbiology Letters; press 25 Feb 2026). Africa, parts of Asia, boreal systems, drylands, mangroves, tundra, and urban soils are underrepresented. A separate SPUN model (~110 quadrillion km of hyphae in topsoil, from >16,000 cores) is a model. It will travel farther than the sampling caveat. That is a discourse risk, not a tape measure.
- **Animals.** October stopped at undescribed insects and refused an extinction year. Different bottleneck: about 1.2% of ~1 million *described* insect species have an extinction-risk assessment (University of Helsinki / Roslin, 20 Aug 2025), and a Science review (4 Apr 2025, DOI 10.1126/science.adq2110) says the global state of insect biodiversity remains unclear. About 80% of insect species are still undescribed. Insect sentience is unproved. Adult Diptera and Blattodea meeting six of Birch's precautionary pain criteria (Gibbons et al. 2022) is not a demonstration of sentience.

Detail on a prior, not a new phylum: Prasoodanan, Maistrenko, Fullam, Mende, Kartal, Coelho, Spang, Bork, Schmidt, Nature Microbiology, 3 Apr 2026 (s41564-026-02314-6), on 92,187 metagenomes. Headline clustering ~705,000 bacterial and ~27,000 archaeal species-level clades; conservative non-singletons ~249,000 and ~12,800. About ten undescribed archaeal and 145 undescribed bacterial phyla are *discoverable in that dataset*, which August already quoted. What August's short page did not carry: isolate genomes plus MAGs capture only 20–50% of contig-visible species; soils, wetlands, rhizosphere, freshwater, and non-mammalian non-avian guts have discovery coefficient α ≥ 0.8; human mouth, urogenital tract, and adult gut approach saturation for *cultivated* diversity while human samples cover only a small fraction of global clades. The human gut is the wrong window for the next phylum and, via FUGAsseM, a plausible window for functions of proteins already sequenced.

Deep-biosphere origin (deep-first vs surface-first), dispersal, and dominant energy source (methane, hydrogen, or radiolysis) remain open and are not a 2026 result. A vent-province effort mismatch was seen only as a summary; the paper was not read; it is not for policy use.

## Individual unproved sentences

- **Space.** Whether any organic molecule in an Enceladus grain or a Europa ice shell is biological is unproved.
- **Earth interior.** Whether deep life started in the crust and moved up, or at the surface and moved down, is unproved, as is the dominant energy source.
- **Ocean.** Whether mesopelagic fishes move closer to 0.5% or closer to 18% of the biological carbon pump at 200 m, in the basin where this was actually bounded, is unproved. Separately, 71.3% of the seafloor still lacks modern-standard bathymetry. That is a map, not a flux.
- **Land.** Whether dryland, boreal, African, and Asian arbuscular-mycorrhizal communities match models trained on temperate samples is unproved, because most ecoregions have no sequences.
- **Animals.** Whether insects as a whole are declining is unproved at global scale. Whether any insect is sentient is unproved.
- **Humans.** What most gut-microbiome proteins do is unproved. Sequence catalogs and structure-guided predictions do not close it.
- **Cross-realm, labeled as a hypothesis of this session, not a measurement.** The next large correction to a planetary budget is more likely to come from converting measurements already in hand (acoustics to sequestered carbon, plume molecules to a biogenicity test, proteins to demonstrated reactions) than from the next 1.4 points of seafloor bathymetry.
