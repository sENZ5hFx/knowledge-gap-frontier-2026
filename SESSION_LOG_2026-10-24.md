## 4. DEEPSEARCH — research brief

**Robust A — attention inversion (counted).**
Inside one corpus of 20,736 papers on 14 tipping elements (2000–2025):

| Element | Papers | Share | Cited threshold (author essay) | Attention deficit vs equal share (1,481 papers) |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Greenland Ice Sheet | 4,141 | 20.0% | ~1.5 °C | 0.000 (above equal share) |
| North Atlantic subpolar gyre | 649 | 3.1% | ~1.8 °C | 0.562 |
| Abrupt boreal permafrost thaw | 139 | 0.7% | ~1.5 °C | 0.906 |

Dynamics studies, same essay: 22 on abrupt boreal thaw, 53 on the subpolar gyre, across 26 years. The authors report no systematic trend that a lower estimated threshold receives more papers.

This does **not** prove boreal permafrost will tip at 1.5 °C. It proves the literature does not allocate itself by the thresholds it cites. Greenland and boreal thaw sit in the same cited band and differ by ~30× in paper count (4,141 / 139).

**Robust B — erasure before cause (observation split from mechanism).**
DOI 10.1073/pnas.2616554123, page text:

- Fishes −63.5%, macrocrustaceans −62.0%, small cephalopods −59.7%, 2011 baseline vs 2015–2023, no return.
- Krill (Euphausiidae) −72.8%, lanternfishes (Myctophidae) −62.9%, *Cyclothone* −69.0%.
- Abstract's cross-taxon range: 60–85% within 3.5 years, then sustained more than a decade.
- Temperature, oxygen, and food supply did not correlate. Deepwater Horizon is called the most parsimonious anthropogenic hypothesis.

The decline numbers are the paper's result. The cause is not demonstrated. Saying "the spill did this" is the conflation this session is built to refuse. The prior logs (latest 2026-08-03) predate the paper. They describe the deep pelagic as unsampled life. This paper describes, in one ecosystem, life that has already dropped. Those are different gaps. Sampling-incompleteness rankings cannot say "the absence already happened."

**Robust C — institutional illegibility, where the fraction is published.**
- 83% of ectomycorrhizal species: sequence without a taxonomic name (secondary).
- 75–99% of gut viral reads: no alignment to a known genome (review, quoting primary catalogues).
- Insect description rate vs estimated total: centuries, per the npj Biodiversity arithmetic.

Unnamed sequences and unnamed species do not enter species-protection lists. The bottleneck is not only collection technology. It is that law and models require an identifier. Prior logs scored "discoverable volume." They did not score "cannot be protected because it has no name."

## 6. CODE EXECUTION

Script logic (Python 3), executed this session. Output file: `identity_deficit_2026-10-24.json` alongside this log in the analysis environment.

Definitions, so the weights are inspectable:

- `attention_deficit = max(0, 1 - papers / (20736/14))`, only if the gap is inside that corpus. Otherwise null. Not imputed.
- `IDI = U * E * L * M * N`
  - U: unidentified fraction. For boreal thaw, U = 1 − 22/139 = 0.8417 (share of mention-papers that are not dynamics studies). This is a coverage ratio, **not** a fraction of permafrost that is unknown.
  - E: erasure weight. already=1.0, imminent_unquantified=0.75, unknown_rate=0.45, not_erasure=0.15. These weights are chosen, not measured.
  - L: 1.0 if a nameless object cannot enter law, lists, or operational models; else 0.35.
  - M: measurability now, 0–1, analyst-set.
  - N: 1.0 if absent from the Jul 27–Aug 3 logs, else 0.25.

Counted attention deficits (robust, no IDI weights):

- Greenland 0.000
- Subpolar gyre 0.562
- Boreal abrupt thaw 0.906

## 11. Primary insight

**The unknown that matters, and that the earlier logs could not see, is not an undiscovered stock of life or a new particle. It is identity arriving after the loss, or not arriving at all.**

Two robust pieces:

1. In the tipping-element literature, attention does not follow the cited thresholds. Greenland and abrupt boreal thaw share a ~1.5 °C cited band and do not share a literature (4,141 papers vs 139).
2. In one deep-pelagic ecosystem, the animals have already declined by ~60–70% depending on taxon and have not come back. The cause has no demonstration. You cannot discover, later, the individuals that are already absent from the nets.

Everything else this session touched (gut viruses, dark fungal taxa, insect names, dark oxygen, black-hole seeds, dark matter) is either a published unnamed-fraction, a dispute, or a famous open problem. None of it is a discovery made here.

## Capability gap identified

No current tool takes a paper corpus and a time series and refuses to promote a correlated story (a spill, a nodule, a threshold) into an identified cause. The refusal had to be written as a function whose undocumented branch returns "undetermined" instead of a number.
