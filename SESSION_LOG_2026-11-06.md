## Primary insight

On 6 Nov 2026 the live error is a collapse of three different oxygen sentences into one "life without sunlight" story.

| Sentence | Kind | What this session may say |
|---|---|---|
| Abyssal chamber oxygen rise (Sweetman and colleagues, 2024, as described by later critiques) | Local dissolved-oxygen time series inside a benthic chamber, hours to days | Disputed. Not admitted to any index. |
| Paleozoic deep-ocean redox (secondary report, 4 Nov 2026, of a Science Advances thallium dataset; Ostrander named there; PDF not opened) | Basin-scale proxy over 10^8 years | The old "switch flipped at 540 Ma" claim is too early by at least ~160 million years on that report. Stable deep oxygen is only bounded as "after about 380 Ma." One sample per ~450,000 years. Not a mechanism for a modern chamber. |
| Hadal seep animals (Peng et al., Nature 2025, 645:679–685; a later southern-Pacific trench said to be similar) | Methane and sulfide chemosynthesis | Animals without sunlight, at those sites. Not oxygen production. Two trenches are not a global fraction. |
| Surface photosynthesis | Oxygenic photosynthesis | Established in sunlit water. Not a license for the chamber and not a date for the abyss. |

`oxygen_kind_gate` returns `KIND_COLLAPSE` for chamber-vs-proxy, chamber-vs-seep, and chamber-vs-photosynthesis, and `SOURCE_ALREADY_SEPARATES` for seep-vs-sunlight, because Peng et al. already use a different chemistry. Nothing is minted.

## One unproved sentence per realm

Each sentence is a refusal or an open bound. None is a finding of this session about nature.

- **Outer space.** No sentence retrieved today moves dark matter, dark energy, or the origin of life. Enceladus and Europa biogenicity stay with the 2026-11-05 log.
- **Earth interior.** Direct radial reach of the deepest borehole is about 0.2% of the mean radius. The mantle and core are not sampled by that ratio.
- **Earth history.** When the deep ocean became stably oxic is unproved past the bound "after about 380 million years ago" in the secondary report. The proxy does not vouch for modern nodule electrolysis.
- **Ocean, chemistry.** Whether any abyssal oxygen rise survives hydrogen, a sufficient in-situ voltage, and a nodule-absent control that does not rise is unproved.
- **Ocean, animals.** Hadal seep animals at the reported trenches are not evidence of oxygen production. A global corridor fraction is unproved.
- **Land.** Whether soil communities carry a memory distinct from leftover DNA and spore banks is unproved. No denominator was admissible.
- **Animals, named.** A reported haul of 57 species new to science from the Mar del Plata Canyon (secondary, 6 Oct 2026) is a naming event. It does not supply a natural history, and it is not this session's lead.
